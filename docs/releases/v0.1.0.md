# LancerLogin Guest Visits plugin v0.1.0

This first release of Guest Visits lets a team publish when guest teams may visit its shop and lets those teams book without an account. Guests see open times for the next eight weeks and which teams are coming, book a visit, and change or cancel it through the link in their confirmation email. Staff manage availability and visits in the dashboard, get Discord messages and a weekly summary, report on visits and, with Outreach, record visits as Team Support. It requires a LancerLogin Core release with module SDK 1.1.0 and a verified Resend connection.

## Bugs fixed

None.

## Features added

- **Guest page** at `/visit`: open and canceled windows for the next eight weeks, the teams already coming with their times and headcount, and a phone-first booking form. A known team number fills in the saved team name, and a repeated submission never books twice.
- **Manage page** at `/visit/manage`, reached only from the emailed link: change the date, times, headcount, note, contact name or phone, or cancel. Opening the link changes nothing. Each link works until the visit ends, and correcting the email address revokes earlier links.
- **Confirmation, update and cancellation emails** through Core's Resend connection, naming your team, with a reply-to address and at most ten Guest Visits emails per address per day.
- **Staff workspace** at `/visits` for Admins and assigned Operators: repeating and one-off availability windows, cancel and restore with a reason, Upcoming, Past and Removed visits, edits, cancellation, removal, restore from change history, resend confirmation, Needs attention and guest teams with merge.
- **Discord messages** for bookings, changes and cancellations, and a **weekly summary** of upcoming visits at a chosen weekday and time (default Monday 12:00 PM), posted once per week even across retries.
- **Guest visits report dataset** in Reports, with period filter and CSV export.
- **Outreach link** (optional): with an Admin's grant in **Settings > Modules > Module access**, every booked visit is recorded as Outreach Team Support on its date; **Record in Outreach** records older visits or retries, with or without a grant.
- **Guided setup** with a test email and the first availability window, **Settings > Guest Visits** for the guest page, email and Discord, and backups that keep visits while dropping every manage link.

## Features removed

None.
