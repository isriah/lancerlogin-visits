# LancerLogin Guest Visits plugin v0.2.0

Guest teams now book from a calendar, and only need their team number. The guest page shows a month at a time with the days that have open times highlighted. Choosing a day opens a card over the calendar, a full-screen sheet on phones, with that day's time slots and the teams already coming, the guest's arrival and departure, their team, contact details and team needs. When an Admin saves FIRST Events API credentials, the team number is looked up in FIRST's FRC and FTC team lists to fill in the program and team name. Bookings, emails, Discord messages and the manage page otherwise work as before.

## Bugs fixed

None.

## Features added

- The guest page is a month calendar instead of a list of times. Days with open times are highlighted and show their hours, or how many time slots they have, on wider screens; on phones they carry a dot. A day whose times were all canceled opens to show the reason, and other days cannot be chosen. Arrow keys move between the days that can be chosen.
- Choosing a day opens a booking card over the calendar. A guest taps a time slot to choose it (a day with one slot chooses it automatically), then picks arrival and departure inside it. Closing the card keeps what the guest typed, and the booking confirmation shows in the card.
- **Team lookup.** Guests type their team number. With one match, the card confirms the program and team name; when several programs use the number, the guest picks their team; with no match, or when lookup is not available, the guest chooses the program and types the name. **Not your team?** always lets them type it instead. FIRST LEGO League teams are entered by hand. An Admin saves a username and token for each program in **Settings > Guest Visits > Team lookup**; tokens are stored encrypted and never shown again. Only the team number is sent to FIRST, answers are kept for seven days, and calls to FIRST are limited to 30 a minute. Outreach records visits with the confirmed program and team name.
- The guest's note is now called **Team needs** everywhere: the booking and manage pages, staff screens, emails and change history. Its help text gives examples such as programming help, CAD review, machine shop access or practice field time.

## Features removed

- The guest page and the setup step no longer show the installation's time zone. Times are still shown in that time zone.

## Required actions

1. To turn on team lookup, register for FIRST's Events API ([FRC](https://frc-events.firstinspires.org/services/API), [FTC](https://ftc-events.firstinspires.org/services/API)) and save each username and token in **Settings > Guest Visits > Team lookup**. Without them, guests choose their program and type their team name as before.
