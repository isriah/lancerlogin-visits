# LancerLogin Guest Visits plugin v0.2.0

Guest teams now book from a calendar. The guest page shows a month at a time for the weeks they can book, with the days that have open times highlighted. A guest chooses a day, sees that day's time slots and the teams already coming, picks a slot and when they will arrive and leave, then fills in their team and contact details. Bookings, emails, Discord messages and the manage page work as before, and the update needs no action.

## Bugs fixed

None.

## Features added

- The guest page is a month calendar instead of a list of times. Days with open times are highlighted and show their hours, or how many time slots they have, on wider screens; on phones they carry a dot. A day whose times were all canceled opens to show the reason, and other days cannot be chosen. Arrow keys move between the days that can be chosen.
- Choosing a day opens its time slots with the teams coming. A guest taps a slot to choose it, and a day with one slot chooses it automatically. Arrival and departure are then chosen inside that slot.

## Features removed

- The guest page and the setup step no longer show the installation's time zone. Times are still shown in that time zone.
